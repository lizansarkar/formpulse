1. Executive Summary & Problem Solved

Traditional online forms suffer from high abandonment rates (over 65% on complex forms) due to long walls of input fields, poor mobile ergonomics, and cold user experiences. FormPulse reinvents form submission into an intuitive, one-question-at-a-time conversational chatbot experience. It empowers teams to visually design forms, customize personality/branding, embed the floating widget on external sites via a 1-line script tag, and collect structured responses in real time.
